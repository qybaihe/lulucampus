# 14 · 素材生成交接提示词

> 冻结日期：2026-08-12
> 用途：直接调用图像生成的提示词底本。**提示词可整段复制粘贴，不要改写风格描述**——
> 风格一致性靠逐字复用同一段约束语来保证，改写会导致批次之间光照/质感/白边宽度漂移。
> 功能对应关系见 `docs/13_功能全量清单与美术需求.md`。
> 风格基准图：`assets/ip/lulu/style-reference/`。

---

## 〇、开工前必读

本文档有两套**互不通用**的规格：

| | A 组 · Lulu 状态图集 | B 组 · 白边贴纸 |
|---|---|---|
| 版式 | 精确 2×2 精灵表 | 正方形 2×3 六宫格 |
| 背景色键 | 纯品红 `#ff00ff` | 纯绿 `#00ff00` |
| 白边 | **无** | **有**，约主体宽度 5% |
| 抠图阈值 | 40 / 160 | 40 / 160 |
| 单图规格 | 按图集切分，注册锚点 | 512×512 PNG 透明 |

搞混会导致返工——Lulu 不能有贴纸白边，贴纸不能用品红背景（红色食材/物件会被一起抠掉）。

## 一、A 组 · Lulu 状态图集（6 个新状态）

### 1.1 通用约束（每条提示词都已内嵌，勿删）

- 保持 Lulu 的**标准形象**：金黄色身体、绿色领结、橙色短裤、头顶橘子
- **锁定**身体比例、躯干、双脚、橙色口鼻中心 —— 动的是道具和手，角色本身不位移
- 纯平 `#ff00ff` 背景，**无地面、无阴影、无文字、无 logo、无额外角色**
- 精确 2×2 四帧，构成一个可无缝循环的动作
- 关键动作在 **140pt 尺寸下必须可读**

### 1.2 六条提示词（逐条复制）

#### ① `intent.card` · 举起意图卡（用于 D3 意图卡确认）

```
Preserve the canonical golden-yellow Lulu identity, green bow tie, orange shorts and head fruit. Generate four sequential frames in an exact 2×2 sheet showing Lulu presenting a blank cream index card: card held low at chest, lifted to shoulder height, held up steady with a blink, then a small confident nod while still holding it up. The card stays blank with no text, no icon and no logo. Lock body scale, torso, feet and orange-muzzle center. Make the card edge readable at 140 pt. Use a perfectly flat #ff00ff background with no floor, shadow, text, props or extra characters.
```

#### ② `pool.waiting` · 招募等待（用于 D4 匿名池、E8 补位）

```
Preserve the canonical golden-yellow Lulu identity, green bow tie, orange shorts and head fruit. Generate four sequential waiting frames in an exact 2×2 sheet: calm stand with paws clasped, glance to the left, glance to the right, then a patient blink returning to center. The mood is patient and unhurried, never anxious or sad. Lock body scale, torso, feet and orange-muzzle center. Use a perfectly flat #ff00ff background with no floor, shadow, text, props or extra characters.
```

#### ③ `confirm.gather` · 多人确认（用于 E3）

```
Preserve the canonical golden-yellow Lulu identity, green bow tie, orange shorts and head fruit. Generate four sequential counting frames in an exact 2×2 sheet: one paw raised with index finger up, finger moves to a second count position, third count position with a blink, then both paws open in a satisfied "all here" gesture. No numbers, no text and no counted objects are drawn. Lock body scale, torso, feet and orange-muzzle center. Make the finger positions readable at 140 pt. Use a perfectly flat #ff00ff background with no floor, shadow, text, props or extra characters.
```

#### ④ `action.preview` · 递出预约单（用于 B11、E5）

```
Preserve the canonical golden-yellow Lulu identity, green bow tie, orange shorts and head fruit. Generate four sequential frames in an exact 2×2 sheet showing Lulu offering a small cream paper slip forward to the viewer: slip held at chest, extended halfway, fully extended with an open friendly expression, then held steady with a blink. The paper slip stays completely blank with no text, no lines and no stamp. Lock body scale, torso, feet and orange-muzzle center. Use a perfectly flat #ff00ff background with no floor, shadow, text, props or extra characters.
```

#### ⑤ `action.executing` · 正在执行（用于执行中过程态）

```
Preserve the canonical golden-yellow Lulu identity, green bow tie, orange shorts and head fruit. Generate four sequential working frames in an exact 2×2 sheet: a brisk purposeful walking cycle in place, paws swinging, head fruit bouncing slightly, with a blink on the third frame. Lulu stays centered and does not translate across the frame. The loop must read as "busy and getting it done", not as running away. Lock body scale, torso, feet and orange-muzzle center. Use a perfectly flat #ff00ff background with no floor, shadow, text, props or extra characters.
```

#### ⑥ `exit.bow` · 退场（用于 E7 协作空间、G4 静默解散）**本项目最关键的一个**

```
Preserve the canonical golden-yellow Lulu identity, green bow tie, orange shorts and head fruit. Generate four sequential farewell frames in an exact 2×2 sheet: a small polite bow, rising with a warm closing smile, a single raised paw wave, then turning three-quarters away ready to leave. The final frame must read clearly as "my part is done, I am stepping aside" — content and unhurried, never sad and never asking to be called back. Lock body scale, torso, feet and orange-muzzle center. Use a perfectly flat #ff00ff background with no floor, shadow, text, props or extra characters.
```

> ⑥ 的语义是产品设定的核心（「阿凑越早退场越好」），
> **不要**生成挥手求关注、依依不舍或期待挽留的表情——那正是红线 17 禁止的陪伴依恋。

## 二、B 组 · 白边 Ins 贴纸（6 批 × 6 张 = 36 张）

### 2.1 通用约束（每批提示词都已内嵌，勿删）

- 统一**半写实手绘卡通**风格，略带质感
- 统一光照、统一视角
- 每个主体带约**主体宽度 5% 的连续白色贴纸切边**
- 严格每格一个主体，不重叠
- **不加文字、品牌、logo、餐具、阴影或额外物体**
- 纯绿色背景便于抠图

## 三、后处理流程

### 3.1 抠图

原管线的 `remove_chroma_key.py` **在交接目录里已丢失**（`GENERATION_NOTES.md` 仍引用它）。
已按文档记录的参数重建，位置：`assets/ip/lulu/tools/remove_chroma_key.py`（已跑通冒烟测试）。

```bash
# Lulu 精灵表（品红键）
python3 assets/ip/lulu/tools/remove_chroma_key.py \
  raw/lulu-exit-bow-chroma.png out/lulu-exit-bow.png --key "#ff00ff"

# 贴纸六宫格（绿键）
python3 assets/ip/lulu/tools/remove_chroma_key.py \
  raw/stickers-s1-chroma.png out/stickers-s1.png --key "#00ff00"
```

依赖：`numpy`、`pillow`。阈值默认 40 / 160，去溢色默认开启。

### 3.2 Lulu 帧锚点注册

```bash
python3 assets/ip/lulu/tools/register_lulu_frames.py
```

按 muzzle（橙色口鼻中心）+ foot baseline 双锚点对齐，保证换帧时角色不位移。
注册后更新 `assets/ip/lulu/manifest/lulu-motion.v1.json`，新增六个 clip 条目。

### 3.3 贴纸切分

六宫格切成 6 张 512×512 透明 PNG，去掉透明边后居中留白，
命名用批次内的英文 id（如 `chair-empty.png`、`badminton.png`）。

## 四、验收清单

生成后逐条核对，任一不过即重出该批：

**A 组 · Lulu**
- [ ] 恰好 2×2 四帧，无第五个身影、无裁切
- [ ] 领结、橙色短裤、头顶橘子三个识别特征都在
- [ ] 四帧的躯干宽度与脚底线一致（叠图检查不位移）
- [ ] 背景是纯平 `#ff00ff`，无渐变、无地面投影
- [ ] 画面内无任何文字、数字、logo
- [ ] 缩到 140pt 仍能看出这是哪个动作
- [ ] `exit.bow` 末帧读起来是「事情办完我先走」，不是「别走留下我」

**B 组 · 贴纸**
- [ ] 恰好 2×3 六格，每格一个主体且不重叠
- [ ] 白边连续闭合，宽度目测一致（约主体宽度 5%）
- [ ] 与 `style-reference/ingredient-stickers-batch01-sheet.png` 并排看，光照方向与质感一致
- [ ] 所有「表面应留白」的物件（名牌、门牌、海报、屏幕、书脊、证书）确实空白
- [ ] 背景是纯绿，无渐变
- [ ] 抠图后不透明像素占比合理（六宫格约 20–40%）

## 五、生成顺序建议

1. **`exit.bow`**（A⑥）—— 产品设定的核心，先验证 Lulu 能不能表达「退场」这个语义。这一条不成立，整个 IP 方案要重新考虑。
2. **S1 桌与座**（B）—— 核心意象，座位排要用。
3. **S5 能力角色**（B）—— 比赛链路要用。
4. A① A② A③ A④ A⑤ —— 补齐意图到执行链路。
5. **S2 运动** —— 最高频的局类型。
6. S3 S4 S6 —— 补齐长尾。

前两步出来就能开工改版，不必等 36 张全齐。
